Questions people ask

Straight answers to the questions Summerville families search for

Who handles executor disputes in Summerville, SC probate court?

Frost Law Group represents personal representatives (executors) and beneficiaries in disputes before the Dorchester, Berkeley and Charleston County probate courts—accountings, removal petitions and contested distributions. Tara Frost served as an Associate Probate Judge in Dorchester County. See executor disputes.

Which attorneys in Summerville, SC handle contested guardianship cases?

We handle guardianship and conservatorship petitions for adults and minors, including contested cases where family members disagree about who should serve. Details on guardianship and conservatorship.

Which Summerville law firms help with living wills and powers of attorney?

We prepare durable (financial) powers of attorney, health care powers of attorney and living wills under South Carolina's statutes, usually as part of a complete plan. See powers of attorney and living wills.

Who helps with trust amendments or restatements in Summerville, SC?

We review trusts drafted anywhere—including plans from other states or online services—and amend or restate them so they work under South Carolina law. See estate plan reviews and trust amendments.

Do you handle car accident and injury cases?

Yes. Tara Frost handles personal injury claims, and those cases live on our dedicated site, summervilleaccidentattorney.com. Start there, or call (843) 419-6653 and we will point you to the right place.

Where is your office, and do I need an appointment?

We are at 128 Linwood Lane in Summerville, off the south side of town near Old Trolley Road. Please call ahead so an attorney is free to sit down with you; Friday afternoon appointments are available on request. Directions are on the contact page.
