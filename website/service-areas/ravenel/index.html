Attorneys Serving Ravenel, SC

Estate planning, probate, guardianship and criminal defense for Ravenel families, from a husband-and-wife firm in Summerville.

Schedule a consultation(843) 419-6653

Ravenel, on Highway 17 south of Charleston, is about forty minutes from our office by Highway 165 and Highway 61. Its families are Charleston County for probate and General Sessions purposes, and its rural land brings the same heirs' property and estate questions we see on Johns Island and in upper Dorchester County.

Where a Ravenel case is heard

Charleston County's probate court and judicial center are on Broad Street in downtown Charleston; bond hearings are held at the detention center in North Charleston. The county is its own judicial circuit, so a Charleston County charge is prosecuted by a different solicitor's office than a Dorchester or Berkeley charge.

Ravenel's town police cases go to the town's municipal court; county cases to the Charleston County magistrates.

How we help Ravenel families

  • Estate planning — wills, revocable trusts, powers of attorney and living wills drafted for South Carolina law and recorded in Charleston County where required.
  • Probate and estate administration — opening, administering and closing estates in the Charleston County Probate Court, small-estate affidavits, and disputes when they arise.
  • Guardianship and conservatorship — for an aging parent or a minor, contested or not.
  • Criminal defense — bond hearings at the Sheriff Al Cannon Detention Center in North Charleston, misdemeanors in magistrate and municipal court, and felonies in Charleston County General Sessions, defended by a former officer.
  • Personal injury — car, truck and motorcycle crashes and dog bites, through our dedicated injury site.

Getting to our office from Ravenel

Follow SC-165 to Bacons Bridge Road, then Old Trolley Road (SC-165), East Carolina Avenue, and turn onto Linwood Lane. The drive is about 21 miles and 38 minutes in normal traffic.

Questions people ask

Which probate court handles an estate for a Ravenel address?

The Charleston County Probate Court in Charleston. Estates are opened in the county where the person lived at death.

Where is the bond hearing after an arrest in Ravenel?

At the Sheriff Al Cannon Detention Center in North Charleston, usually within 24 hours of a warrant arrest.

Do you meet clients in Ravenel?

Consultations are at our Summerville office or by phone and video. For estate-plan signings we can sometimes arrange a meeting closer to you; ask when you call.
