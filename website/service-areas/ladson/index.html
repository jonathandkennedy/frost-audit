Ladson sits where Dorchester, Berkeley and Charleston counties meet, which makes it the community where “which court?” is asked most often. College Park Road, Ladson Road and the Exchange Park fairgrounds are ten minutes from our office, and we sort out the county question on the first call.

Where a Ladson case is heard

Dorchester County's courthouse complex—the probate court, the clerk of court and General Sessions (felony) court—is in St. George, about 28 miles up Highway 78 from Summerville. The county magistrates sit in St. George and at the Troy Knight Judicial Complex on Deming Way in Summerville, and bond hearings are held at the county detention center on Hodge Road in Summerville.

Ladson is unincorporated, so there is no municipal court; charges go to the magistrate of the county where the stop or arrest happened.

Which county is your Ladson address in?

A Ladson estate is probated in the county where the person lived—Dorchester for addresses off Ladson Road toward Summerville, Berkeley for the College Park Road corridor, Charleston for the southern edge. The county on the property tax bill controls. We check it before filing so the family does not lose weeks to a refiling.

How we help Ladson families

  • Estate planning — wills, revocable trusts, powers of attorney and living wills drafted for South Carolina law and recorded in Dorchester County where required.
  • Probate and estate administration — opening, administering and closing estates in the Dorchester County Probate Court, small-estate affidavits, and disputes when they arise.
  • Guardianship and conservatorship — for an aging parent or a minor, contested or not.
  • Criminal defense — bond hearings at the Dorchester County Detention Center on Hodge Road in Summerville, misdemeanors in magistrate and municipal court, and felonies in Dorchester County General Sessions, defended by a former officer.

Questions people ask

Which probate court handles an estate for a Ladson address?

The Dorchester County Probate Court in St. George. Estates are opened in the county where the person lived at death.

Where is the bond hearing after an arrest in Ladson?

County arrests are heard at the Dorchester County Detention Center on Hodge Road in Summerville, usually within 24 hours. Municipal charges are heard by the municipal judge for that town.

Do you meet clients in Ladson?

Consultations are at our Summerville office or by phone and video. For estate-plan signings we can sometimes arrange a meeting closer to you; ask when you call.
